Your family's records, in one safe place.

Family Archive helps parents write down what the family owns and owes: land, bank accounts, fixed deposits, insurance, loans and disputes. When the time comes, their children know what exists, where the papers are, and how to claim it.

What it does

यह क्या करता है

Record

Fill in simple forms, photograph a passbook or policy, or speak a voice note in Hindi.

फ़ॉर्म भरें, कागज़ की फ़ोटो लें, या हिंदी में बोलकर बताएं।

Check

Suggestions read from your papers wait for your confirmation. Nothing is saved as true until you say so.

काग़ज़ों से पढ़ी गई हर बात आपकी पुष्टि के बाद ही जुड़ती है।

Share

Choose which family members can see what. Sensitive numbers stay sealed and every view is recorded.

आप तय करें कि परिवार में कौन क्या देख सकता है।

Our promises

हमारे वादे

  • We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.
  • Everything you store is encrypted on our servers. Account numbers and other sealed details get a second layer and are never sent to any AI service.
  • Documents are read by AI only when you agree to it for that file.
  • You can download everything, or ask us to delete it, at any time.
