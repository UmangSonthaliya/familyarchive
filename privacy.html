Who we are

Family Archive is a sole proprietorship registered in India (Udyam UDYAM-KR-03-0785731), based in Bengaluru, Karnataka. We run the Family Archive app and this website. In this policy "we" means Family Archive and "you" means anyone who uses the app.

Family Archive is in a private pilot. This policy describes the app as it works today; when that changes, we update this page and the date above.

What we collect

  • Your phone number, to sign you in with a one-time code.
  • Device details: a name for the device, its platform (Android or web) and a security key created on the device, so a stolen sign-in can't be reused elsewhere.
  • What you put in the archive: details of land, homes, bank accounts, deposits, insurance, money lent or borrowed and disputes; the names and relationships of family members; and the photos, PDFs and voice notes you upload.
  • Activity records: when sensitive information is viewed, revealed, downloaded or exported, and by whom, so the family can see who looked at what.

Some of what you record is about other people, such as relatives, nominees or a borrower. Please add only what the family needs, and tell those people where it is reasonable to do so.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your archive, keep its history, check what is missing, and share it with the family members you choose.
  • To sign you in and keep your account safe.
  • To read documents and voice notes you upload, only when you agree to it for that file (see below).

We do not sell your information, we do not show advertising, and we do not use your archive for any other purpose.

Reading documents with AI

When you upload a document or voice note, the app asks whether it may be read automatically. If you agree, that one file is sent to an AI service, which returns suggestions such as an account type, a policy date or an area of land. The suggestions wait in your confirmation queue; nothing is added to your archive until you accept it.

Sealed details (account numbers, policy numbers and similar) are never sent to an AI service. The owner types them in by hand.

You can turn AI reading off for the whole family in the app's settings.

How we protect it

  • Everything in your archive is encrypted on our servers with a key that belongs to your family.
  • Sealed details get a second layer of encryption, are shown only as their last 4 characters, and every time someone reveals one it is recorded.
  • Downloading the full archive or revealing a sealed detail needs a fresh one-time code.
  • Sign-ins are tied to the device, and you can see and sign out any of your devices in the app.

We say this plainly: because the encryption keys are held on our side (so your family can still get in if your phone is lost), we are technically able to decrypt your data. We don't look at it except where you ask us to help, or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your archive for as long as your family's account exists, because its purpose is to be there years later. Sign-in codes expire after 5 minutes and are then useless. Original documents are never changed once uploaded.

Your choices and rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • see and download everything we hold about you (the app's export does this);
  • correct anything that is wrong (every field can be edited, and the history is kept);
  • withdraw consent, for example for AI reading;
  • ask us to delete your account and your family's archive;
  • name someone to act for you if you can no longer do so yourself;
  • complain to us, and if you are not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

To delete your data, write to familyarchive.help@gmail.com from your registered phone's WhatsApp or by email, telling us the phone number on the account. We confirm it is you, then delete the archive within 30 days, except anything the law requires us to keep.

Children

The app is for adults. Children's details may appear in a family's archive (for example as a nominee or heir), but children do not get their own accounts.

Contact and grievances

Questions, requests and complaints go to the proprietor of Family Archive at familyarchive.help@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days.
